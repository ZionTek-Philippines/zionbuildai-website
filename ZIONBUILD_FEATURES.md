## 2. Target Customers

### Primary — Contractors & Construction Companies

General contractors, residential contractors, design-and-build firms, small/medium construction firms, growing companies with multiple projects, project managers, engineers, and construction business owners.

**Pain points:** monitoring multiple projects · unclear profitability · scattered expenses and financial records · attendance and payroll complications · worker vale and cash advances · poor materials monitoring · delayed progress reports · hard-to-verify actual accomplishments · unclear schedules · time-consuming client reporting.

### Secondary — Homeowners

People building or renovating their homes.

**Pain points:** limited visibility into progress · uncertainty about spending · inconsistent photo updates · hard-to-understand completion percentages · unexpected delays · unclear milestones · communication gaps with contractors.

> **Tone rule:** ZionBuild builds trust and transparency *between* contractors and homeowners. Never portray contractors as dishonest or workers as lazy.

## 4. AI Capabilities

**Shipped (confirmed by the product owner, Nov 2026):**

- **AI Photo Analysis**: AI reviews the photos attached to a work log or progress update and suggests which tasks / milestone items they match, with confidence, image quality, evidence coverage, limitations, and a suggested progress range. A person must accept a match before official progress changes; AI never changes progress on its own. May be presented as a working feature.

**Beta (built, still being refined; on the website label it "Available on selected projects", the same as AI Photo Analysis):**

- **AI Progress Validation**: suggests a progress % per milestone item from photos, daily reports, attendance and inventory; a person approves, modifies or rejects it.
- **AI Recommendations**: flags issues (schedule, quality, etc.) with severity, evidence and a recommended action; a person acknowledges or dismisses them.

**Planned / unverified (label "Coming soon"):**

- Schedule risk identification
- Budget risk insights
- Construction productivity analysis
- Project management recommendations
- Construction data insights

**Positioning:** "Practical AI that helps you understand construction data and make better project decisions."

**Never promise:** fully autonomous construction management, guaranteed cost savings, or perfectly accurate AI progress detection.

## 8. Visual Direction

Premium construction-tech SaaS: professional, trustworthy, modern but practical, construction-oriented, mobile-first, credible to Philippine firms, welcoming to homeowners.

**Use:** clean typography, strong headlines, premium dashboard mockups, realistic Philippine construction photography, real product screenshots, financial charts, progress galleries, phone mockups, clear CTAs.

**Avoid:** robot illustrations, excessive AI glow effects, fake futuristic UIs, unreadable screenshots, overcrowded grids, technical jargon, stock photos disconnected from Philippine construction.

Use ZionBuild's actual brand colors and logo when provided — do not invent official brand colors.

## 9. Content Rules (Must Follow)

1. **Do not invent** product capabilities, integrations, certifications, testimonials, customer stories, or performance statistics.
2. **AI features are planned** unless §4 lists them as shipped (AI Photo Analysis) or Beta — label the rest as upcoming.
3. **Bank records ≠ accounting/bank integration.** No auto-reconciliation claims.
4. **Client portal shows authorized information only.**
5. **Never** frame contractors as dishonest or workers as lazy.
6. Dashboard mockup numbers are illustrative sample data, not claims.
